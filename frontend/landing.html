How to use this project

1

Upload Images: Start by uploading your Before (T1) and After (T2) satellite images into the dropzones.

2

Set Threshold: Adjust the confidence threshold slider to filter out weak detections, and toggle the 'Filter Natural Changes' option if needed.

3

Select Sensor Platform: Choose the appropriate satellite sensor profile (e.g., Sentinel-2 MSI (10m) or ISRO LISS-4 (5.8m)) to ensure the AI calibrates to the correct spatial resolution for your images.

4

Run Analysis: Click 'Run Change Detection' to execute the forward pass and process the images through the AI model.

5

Get Output: You will receive a comprehensive analysis including: a visual Heatmap (AI confidence), precise Bounding Boxes around affected areas, the total Anomaly Cluster Count, the Dominant Change Category (e.g., Construction, Excavation), processing metrics (latency/accuracy), and the ability to download a professional GeoJSON or PDF Audit Report.
